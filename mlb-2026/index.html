Method. Every 2026 regular-season pitch from Baseball Savant (716,792 pitches, 2,429 games) plus Savant leaderboards (OAA, framing, baserunning, bat tracking, sprint speed, pitch arsenals, team expected stats 2021-2026). Quality is converted to runs from xwOBA with the 2026 wOBA-to-runs relation across the 30 teams; results vs. contact is tested for repeatability with a 2021-2026 backtest; projections are estimates checked with 200,000 simulated seasons. Roster, contract and injury facts are cited on each page. Tables come from the data; plans and projections are interpretation.

Analysis: Emerson Jimenez, Emerson Performance (EP).
