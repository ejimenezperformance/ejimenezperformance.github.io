How this work is done

01

Public data only.

Every project runs on Baseball Savant / Statcast exports anyone can download — no proprietary feeds, nothing that can't be checked.

02

Negative results get published, not buried.

If a metric doesn't predict what it's supposed to, that's the headline — see the elbow-injury and swing-plane repos.

03

Corrections happen in the open.

A coefficient-scaling bug in an early hitting model was caught, fixed, and documented — then the fix was re-verified across every repo that had cited the old number.

04

Every repo is reproducible.

Data, scripts, and a one-command way to regenerate every chart — bilingual, in English and Spanish.

About

I've spent 15+ years as a Strength & Conditioning Coach across MLB affiliate academies (Pittsburgh Pirates DR Academy), LMB (Acereros de Monclova), and LVBP (Cardenales de Lara). That background is the reason EP-TSP exists: most public baseball analytics stops at the statistic, and most performance coaching stops at the movement. I build the bridge between them.

I'm a SABR member and a participant in the 2026 MLB Pipeline Player Development & Analytics Cohort, currently based in South Florida.
